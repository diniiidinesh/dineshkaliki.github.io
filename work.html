Work Portfolio

Case studies from product management work — the problem, the tradeoffs, and what shipped. More being added over time.

Loco

Building Subscriptions from Zero, Across Six Markets

A multi-phase 0→1 monetization system spanning content gating, flexible creator pricing, payment recovery, and moderation.

Problem

No subscription monetization or payments infrastructure existed.

Strategy

Split consent flows by jurisdiction; built recovery for soft vs. hard payment declines.

Outcome

Shipped in multiple phases; 34-signal instrumentation framework.

 Loco

Quest Engine: From Brazil Pilot to a Self-Serve Growth Lever

A 0→1 gamification system built as the primary activation and retention lever, then iterated hard post-launch.

Problem

Needed an activation/retention lever for the Brazil launch, without relying on push.

Strategy

Configurable Retool backend + personalized, geo-filtered quests.

Outcome

5% task completion in pilot, zero push notifications.

 Junglee Games

Timeline Missions: Moving Retention Across Seven Games

Leading a gamification/CDP platform serving 100mn+ users, and shipping the feature that moved retention, player value, and revenue.

Problem

Engagement wasn't sticky enough across sessions, with revenue-leakage risk in any fix.

Strategy

De-risked via POC first; cross-team sign-off before shipping.

Outcome

Up to 20% retention, 17.2% player value, 21.4% NGR lift.
