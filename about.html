About Me

I grew up in Kurnool, trained as a mechanical engineer, spent two years in a cement plant and then in software support, and found my way to product management through an MBA at XLRI. Since then it's been gaming and live-streaming platforms — Junglee Games, then Loco — building the systems underneath the features: monetization, gamification, and the lifecycle infrastructure teams run on.
